BH06-1Not null, ≤120 characters. Required where a highway is carried.
BH06-3Must appear in the state’s HPMS submittal.
BH06-4Not reported where B.H.18 shows the feature is carried on another bridge.
BH07-1Valid numeric value.
BH07-28 digits maximum.

That is every FHWA check. Nothing tests whether the milepoint is near the route — that is what the QC group is for.

  • BH06 is the route carried on the bridge, never the one below.
  • Concurrent routes on one deck still get a single BH06 / BH07.
  • BH07 is county-scoped — it resets to 0 at every county line and is not the mile marker sign number.
  • Measures follow the road, round every curve. Locate check proves it per bridge.
  • BH07 is handled at three decimals throughout; the unrounded source is kept in the CSV.
